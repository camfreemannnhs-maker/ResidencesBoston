Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Residences Extended Stay different from a normal hotel stay? +
The format is built for monthly living rather than overnight travel. Guests get furnished suites, kitchens, laundry, parking, and a stay structure that works better for healthcare, corporate, relocation, and insurance use cases.
Do you accept insurance and relocation housing assignments? +
Yes. We work directly with insurance companies, third-party relocation management companies (RMCs), and corporate HR departments on fire, flood, and disaster displacement housing, as well as employee relocation programs. Contact our corporate team for direct billing and volume rates.
Is Residences Extended Stay a good fit for travel nurses? +
Absolutely. Our locations in Marlborough MA, Tewksbury MA, and Merrimack NH are within 20–45 minutes of major hospitals including UMass Memorial, Lowell General, St. Joseph's, and Southern NH Medical Center. We offer flexible 4–13 week leases designed for travel nursing assignments, with fully furnished suites, in-unit laundry, and private parking.
What is the minimum stay? +
Our minimum stay is two weeks across all three properties. We offer nightly, weekly, and monthly rates — with the best value on monthly and long-term commitments. Long-term rates (8+ months) are significantly lower than our short-term monthly rates and include the same $400/month flat utility bundle.
Are utilities and Wi-Fi included? +
Yes. All rates include high-speed Wi-Fi, utilities (heat, electric, water), weekly housekeeping service, and on-site parking. There are no hidden fees.
Do you allow pets? +
Yes, we are pet-friendly at select locations. A small pet fee applies. Please contact us to confirm availability and details for the property you're interested in.
