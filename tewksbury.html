Tewksbury's Premier
Extended Stay Address

Situated at 20 International Place, right off I-495 with fast connections to I-93 and I-95, our Tewksbury property puts you at the center of one of New England's most active employment corridors — without the Boston price tag.

We offer studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom/two-bathroom suites — all fully furnished with full-size kitchens (not kitchenettes), in-unit washers and dryers, and free parking. Because we're a hotel, you bring only your suitcase. No security deposit. No furniture rental. No utility setup. Same-day move-in is available when you need it.

Our inflation-proof housing program lets you lock in your monthly rate and utilities at a flat $400 bundle — electricity, gas, water, heat, hot water, A/C, trash removal, cable, Wi-Fi, and phone — so your monthly cost never surprises you.

Travel nurses on assignment at Lowell General Hospital, Lahey Hospital, and surrounding facilities choose us because we're practical: close to work, quiet, with parking at the door. Corporate teams, relocating employees, and insurance-displacement families choose us for the same reason. Add in a pool, patio, business center, sundry convenience, and a staffed front desk open daily, and the stay starts to feel far more complete than a generic long-stay hotel room.

Where exactly is Residences at International Place? +
We are located at 20 International Place, Tewksbury, MA 01876 — directly off I-495, five minutes from I-93, and fifteen minutes from I-95. Easy to reach from Burlington, Woburn, Chelmsford, Lowell, and the entire north suburban Boston corridor.
What is the minimum stay? +
Our minimum stay is two weeks. We offer nightly, weekly, and monthly rates — with the best value on monthly and long-term commitments. Extended stay rates (8+ months) are significantly lower than our short-term monthly rates.
What is included in the $400 utility bundle? +
The $400/month utility bundle includes electricity, gas, water, heat, hot water, air conditioning, trash removal, and cable TV with 70+ channels. This flat rate locks in your costs so you know exactly what you'll pay each month — no surprise bills.
Can I book corporate or insurance billing here? +
Absolutely. We work regularly with HR departments, relocation management companies, and insurance carriers for ALE (Additional Living Expense) assignments. Contact our team at (978) 863-9800 or visit our Corporate page for details.
Are pets allowed at the Tewksbury location? +
Yes. Pets are welcome. Cats are $25/month additional and dogs are $50/month additional. Please contact us with any specific questions about our pet policy.
