Corporate FAQ

Questions from HR & Procurement

How quickly can you set up direct billing? +
We can set up a direct billing account within 24–48 business hours for most companies. We'll need a billing contact, PO number or cost center, and billing address. We issue monthly consolidated invoices and can accommodate custom billing formats for most RMC and Fortune 500 workflows.
Do you work with RMCs and relocation platforms? +
Yes. We work regularly with relocation management companies and are familiar with the major RMC platforms and approval workflows. We can supply preferred supplier agreements, W-9s, certificates of insurance, and other supplier onboarding documentation on request.
How do you handle insurance ALE assignments? +
We're experienced with Additional Living Expense (ALE) and Loss of Use housing assignments. We can often accommodate policyholders with 24–48 hours notice, provide furnished suites that meet ALE standards, and bill the carrier directly. Our team coordinates directly with adjusters to simplify the process.
What are the volume discounts for corporate accounts? +
Volume discount tiers begin at 2+ simultaneous suites and scale with commitment level. Companies that establish master service agreements with guaranteed volume thresholds receive the most favorable rates. Contact our corporate team for a customized proposal based on your specific program.
