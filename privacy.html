Privacy Policy

Residences Extended Stay uses this website to help prospective guests, partners, and housing coordinators evaluate availability and request accommodations across our portfolio.

Information We Collect

We may collect contact information, stay preferences, company details, and related housing-request information when you submit an inquiry, request a quote, book a consultation, or contact our team. We may also collect standard website usage data such as browser type, referring source, and pages visited.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to housing inquiries, route leads to the appropriate property, coordinate reservations, support corporate or insurance assignments, improve our marketing content, and maintain the security and performance of the site.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that support our website, CRM, scheduling, reservations, analytics, or communications workflows, and with authorized business partners when necessary to fulfill a housing request.

Retention & Security

We retain inquiry and reservation-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage guest communications, business records, and legal obligations. We use commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

If you would like to update, correct, or delete information you have submitted through this site, contact our team and we will respond in a commercially reasonable timeframe, subject to any legal or operational requirements that apply.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through our contact page. This policy may be updated from time to time as the site, services, or compliance requirements evolve.
