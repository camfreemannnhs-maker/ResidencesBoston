Before You Reach Out

Common Questions

How fast will you respond to my inquiry? +
We respond to all online inquiries within two business hours. For urgent situations — insurance displacement, emergency corporate housing, or same-day move-in — call the property directly. All three properties are staffed 8 AM to 8 PM, seven days a week.
Can I move in the same day I contact you? +
Yes. Because we operate as a hotel, your suite can be ready the same day in most cases. Call us directly and we will confirm availability within the hour. All you need to bring is your suitcase — suites are fully furnished with everything you need from day one.
How do I set up direct billing for my company? +
Fill out the form above and select "Corporate / Business" or call us directly at any location. We can set up direct billing accounts within 24–48 business hours. We accommodate most RMC platforms and Fortune 500 procurement workflows. We can provide W-9s, certificates of insurance, and preferred supplier agreements on request.
Which location should I choose? +
Choose based on your assignment: Marlborough is best for MetroWest and Worcester-area needs. Tewksbury is best for greater Lowell, North Shore, and Boston-accessible assignments. Merrimack is best for southern New Hampshire — Nashua, Manchester, and Fidelity Merrimack area.
Is there a minimum stay requirement? +
Our minimum stay is two weeks. We offer nightly, weekly, and monthly rates — with the best value on monthly and long-term commitments. Long-term rates (8+ months) come with a flat $400/month utility bundle and significantly lower base rates than our short-term monthly pricing.
