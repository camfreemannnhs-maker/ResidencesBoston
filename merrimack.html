Premier Extended Stay Address

Situated at 246 Daniel Webster Highway, right off Rt-3 with fast access to both Nashua and Manchester, our Merrimack property puts you at the heart of one of New Hampshire's most active business corridors — without the high-cost-of-living tax that comes with branded hotel alternatives.

We offer studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom/two-bathroom suites — all fully furnished with full-size kitchens (not kitchenettes), in-unit washers and dryers, and free parking. Because we're a hotel, you bring only your suitcase. No security deposit. No furniture rental. No utility setup. Same-day move-in is available when you need it.

Our inflation-proof housing program lets you lock in your monthly rate and utilities at a flat $400 bundle — electricity, gas, water, heat, hot water, A/C, trash removal, and cable with 70+ channels — so your monthly cost never surprises you. For multi-month and annual guests, that stability is worth as much as the savings.

Fidelity Investments employees, BAE Systems contractors, and consultants on extended Merrimack assignments have made this property their base of operations for years. Travel nurses on assignment at Southern NH Medical Center and St. Joseph Hospital choose us for the proximity and the practicality: parking at your door, a real kitchen, and in-unit laundry so you never have to hunt for a laundromat. Insurance families displaced by fire or flood are welcomed with same-day move-in and no deposit required.

Merrimack FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Where exactly is Residences at Daniel Webster? +
We are located at 246 Daniel Webster Highway, Merrimack, NH 03054 — just off Rt-3. Ten minutes south puts you in downtown Nashua. Fifteen minutes north puts you in Manchester. Fidelity Investments' Merrimack campus is just a few minutes down the road.
What is the minimum stay? +
Our minimum stay is two weeks. We offer nightly, weekly, and monthly rates — with the best value on monthly and long-term commitments. Long-term rates (8+ months) are significantly lower than our short-term monthly rates and include the same $400/month flat utility bundle.
What is included in the $400 utility bundle? +
The $400/month utility bundle includes electricity, gas, water, heat, hot water, air conditioning, trash removal, and cable TV with 70+ channels. This flat rate locks in your costs so you know exactly what you'll pay each month — no surprise electric bills, especially important through New Hampshire winters.
Does Residences at Daniel Webster work with Fidelity's relocation program? +
Yes. We have experience working with HR departments, relocation management companies, and third-party administrators for corporate stays. We can accommodate direct billing and group bookings. Contact our team at (603) 424-8100 or visit our Corporate page for details.
Can I book corporate or insurance billing at the Merrimack location? +
Absolutely. We work regularly with HR departments, relocation management companies, and insurance carriers for ALE (Additional Living Expense) assignments. Same-day move-in is available for emergency displacement situations. Call (603) 424-8100 to coordinate.
Are pets allowed at the Merrimack location? +
Yes. Pets are welcome. Cats are $25/month additional and dogs are $50/month additional. Please contact us with any specific questions about our pet policy at the Daniel Webster property.
