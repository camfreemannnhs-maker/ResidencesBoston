Situated at 880 Donald Lynch Blvd, just off I-290 near Solomon Pond Mall, our Marlborough property puts you at the center of Central Massachusetts' most active employment and healthcare corridor — with straightforward access to Worcester, Framingham, Natick, and the Mass Pike.

We offer studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom/two-bathroom suites — all fully furnished with full-size kitchens (not kitchenettes), in-unit washers and dryers, and free parking. Because we're a hotel, you bring only your suitcase. No security deposit. No furniture rental. No utility setup. Same-day move-in is available when you need it.

Our inflation-proof housing program lets you lock in your monthly rate and utilities at a flat $400 bundle — electricity, gas, water, heat, hot water, A/C, trash removal, and cable with 70+ channels — so your monthly cost never surprises you. For multi-month guests managing their cost per day, that locked rate is a significant advantage.

Travel nurses on assignment at UMass Memorial, Marlborough Hospital, and Milford Regional choose us because the math is clear: a full suite, with a kitchen and in-unit laundry, for hundreds less per month than a Marriott Residence Inn. Corporate teams from Cisco, Boston Scientific, Raytheon, and Bose choose us for the same reason — and because our direct billing makes expense management seamless.

Marlborough FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Where exactly is Residences at Solomon Pond? +
We are located at 880 Donald Lynch Blvd, Marlborough, MA 01752 — directly off I-290, near Solomon Pond Mall and the Route 9 corridor. From here, Worcester is twenty minutes west and Boston is forty-five minutes east. Cisco, Boston Scientific, and Marlborough Hospital are all within ten minutes.
What is the minimum stay? +
Our minimum stay is two weeks. We offer nightly, weekly, and monthly rates — with the best value on monthly and long-term commitments. Long-term rates (8+ months) are significantly lower than our short-term monthly rates and include the same $400/month flat utility bundle.
What is included in the $400 utility bundle? +
The $400/month utility bundle includes electricity, gas, water, heat, hot water, air conditioning, trash removal, and cable TV with 70+ channels. This flat rate locks in your costs so you know exactly what you'll pay each month — no surprise bills, no seasonal heating spikes.
How far is Solomon Pond from UMass Memorial Medical Center? +
UMass Memorial Medical Center in Worcester is approximately 20 minutes from our Marlborough location via I-290. Marlborough Hospital is closer — about 8 minutes. Travel nurses on assignment at either facility find this location practical for the commute and genuinely more livable than conventional hotel alternatives.
Can I book corporate or insurance billing here? +
Absolutely. We work regularly with HR departments, relocation management companies, and insurance carriers for ALE (Additional Living Expense) assignments. Contact our team at (508) 460-1000 or visit our Corporate page for details on direct billing and group arrangements.
Are pets allowed at the Marlborough location? +
Yes. Pets are welcome at Residences at Solomon Pond. Cats are $25/month additional and dogs are $50/month additional. Please contact us at (508) 460-1000 with any specific questions about our pet policy.
