Travel Nurse FAQ

Everything You Want to Know

What is the minimum stay for travel nurses? +
Our minimum stay is two weeks, making us a good fit for both short-contract and standard 13-week travel nursing assignments. Most travel nurse guests book 8-week or 13-week contracts. We can also handle back-to-back contract renewals at the same property — just let us know in advance.
Can I book directly without going through my agency? +
Absolutely. Booking directly with Residences Extended Stay means you receive the housing stipend from your agency and keep the difference between our direct rate and the stipend. Many travel nurses save $300–$600+ per month this way.
What if my contract gets extended or cancelled? +
We understand that travel nursing contracts change. We work with you on extensions when possible, and our cancellation terms are designed with the realities of healthcare contracts in mind. Contact us to discuss the specific terms for your situation.
Is there a dedicated nurse contact for booking? +
Yes. We have a dedicated travel healthcare liaison who handles nurse bookings and understands the unique needs of travel nursing assignments. Reach out by phone or the form below for a fast, personal response.
Do you have availability for multiple nurses at once? +
Yes. If you're part of a travel nursing cohort or your unit has multiple travelers, we can often accommodate groups at the same property. Contact us with your team size and we'll find the best available solution.
